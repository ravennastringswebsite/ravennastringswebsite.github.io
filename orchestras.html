Our Orchestras

We give string players of all levels (age 13 and up) a welcoming, low-pressure place to make music with wonderful people and explore great repertoire — perfect for keeping up your skills or getting back into playing after some time off.

There are no auditions. Choose whichever group suits you. Some folks go back and forth between groups (and some even do both!)

The Ravenna Strings Sinfonia

Mondays, 7–8:15pm · conducted by Nathan Harrison

Beginner/intermediate level, focussing a little more on technique and ensemble playing. Repertoire is chosen from arrangements of medium difficulty or pieces written for intermediate players. We have fun while preparing for the short concert at the end of each term.

The Ravenna Strings

Thursdays, 7–8:15pm · conducted by Joseph Pollard White

Intermediate/advanced level, focussing a little more on repertoire. Rehearsals are informal and low pressure, but we're serious about preparing for the short concert at the end of each term.

Bring a stand!

The Details

Where
Phinney Neighborhood Center, 6532 Phinney Ave. N, Seattle. Parking at the Center and on the street; Metro bus 5.
When
Three terms (Fall, Winter, Spring), each ending in a free joint concert at St. Andrew's Church, 111 NE 80th St. See Concerts & Schedules.
Cost
$150/term or $450/season ($90 / $270 reduced for students, seniors, etc.), or pay what you wish. No one will ever be turned away due to finances. Register

Conductors

Joseph Pollard White, Director (left), from New York, earned his Doctorate in Orchestral Conducting at the University of Washington after studies at Eastman and Indiana University, has taught at Pacific Lutheran, Northern Kentucky and Christopher Newport universities, and is active as a violist, composer and conductor.

Nathan Harrison, Associate Director (right), from Anchorage, Alaska, studied 'cello at the University of Puget Sound, holds a Master's in Music Education from City University, and directs Orchestras and Chamber Music at Kentwood High School.
