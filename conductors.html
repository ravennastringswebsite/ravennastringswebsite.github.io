Our Conductors

Joseph Pollard White

Director · conducts The Ravenna Strings

Originally from New York, Joseph earned his Doctorate in Orchestral Conducting at the University of Washington after studies at Eastman and Indiana University, has taught at Pacific Lutheran, Northern Kentucky and Christopher Newport universities, and is active as a violist, composer and conductor.

Nathan Harrison

Associate Director · conducts The Ravenna Strings Sinfonia

Originally from Anchorage, Alaska, Nathan studied 'cello at the University of Puget Sound, holds a Master's in Music Education from City University, and directs Orchestras and Chamber Music at Kentwood High School.
